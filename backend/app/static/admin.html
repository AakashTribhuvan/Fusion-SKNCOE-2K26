Session controls

Monitor in-memory demo sessions, recover a QR or video step that is genuinely stuck, or deliberately fail a QR test for a supervised demonstration. When shared through Cloudflare, this page and its controls are also reachable through that public URL.

Anyone with the public demo URL can use these passwordless controls. Only enable this during a supervised development demo. Skipping a QR code advances the paired phone but creates no scan evidence; skipping any QR code makes the final report inconclusive. The QR failure control marks the QR check failed in the report. A video override discards screening results and makes the report inconclusive. Actions and reasons are recorded in the report. Disable admin controls when the demo ends.
