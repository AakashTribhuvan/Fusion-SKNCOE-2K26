Optional voice screening

This is a separate, optional test. It checks a fresh spoken phrase and an AI-voice screening signal; it does not identify or match a speaker.

  1. Move to a quiet place and keep the microphone about 15–25 cm away.
  2. After you explicitly consent and start, allow microphone access, then say the displayed three-word phrase once at a natural pace.
  3. Recording stops after eight seconds. Audio is sent for phrase and voice screening, processed in memory, and discarded.
